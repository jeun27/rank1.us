## DO NOT USE (anywhere: copy, fixes, reports)

- 'AI is 7% / 30% of traffic' or any AI traffic share
- 'Organic traffic down 20-40%' as a flat claim
- Webflow '6x' / '8%' figures
- '100% AI-written content is never cited'
- Asking an LLM why it ranked you, or its source weights
- '35% / 70% overlap' figures
- Schema markup as an AI-citation lever (hygiene only, no lift claim)
- Q&A blocks / tables as proven to work
- 'Runs on Bing'
- A fixed '800M users' number
- llms.txt or fresh dates as proven levers (internal ideas only)
- 'Be the one recommended by AI' (rank1.so's line): never use, in any form
- Vendor stats in customer copy: BrightLocal Aug 2026 shares (GBP 45.2%, Yelp 21.8%, TripAdvisor 7.0%, OpenTable 6.6%), Yext 41.6% / 39.8% / 13.3%, SOCi 4.2-4.3 stars / 68% / 47% (engineer why notes only)
- 'Reach 4.3 stars and get cited' or any rating, review or traffic promise
- Restaurant/Menu schema as an AI lever (H1 is a tidy-up only)

## Internal terms (never customer-facing)

- 'Fix-Forward Reporting': internal only; never in UI, emails, reports, exports or landing
- fix_rules.why_note text and vendor names behind it
- Rule keys (A1, R12, H1) are for engineers; customers see the customer_copy only
