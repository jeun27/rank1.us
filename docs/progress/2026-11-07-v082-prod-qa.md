## Findings (non-blocking)

- F1: every load of /app/onboarding first shows an h1 "Dashboard" (recorded h1 sequence: "Dashboard" → "Dashboard+Add your business" → "Add your business"). /app/onboarding has no loading.tsx, so it inherits src/app/app/loading.tsx (`<Skeleton title="Dashboard" />`). A tab that never finishes streaming stays on "Dashboard", which is what the old lead saw in the Mac's in-app tab. Fix: an onboarding loading.tsx titled "Add your business", or a neutral skeleton title.
- F2: the reading log never shows "4 of 4" or the Services result. When readServices resolves, Onboarding sets the result and calls onNext() in the same tick, so the last painted state is "3 of 4 … Reading your menu links…" (DOM observer, 1280). The fourth tick is never seen.
- Note: reading rank1.us (not a restaurant) prefilled cuisine "ramen", most likely from the landing's demo copy. Expected for a non-restaurant site, so not a bug.

Cost: two site reads of rank1.us and two Google Maps competitor lookups (step 3, two runs). No AI calls, no free audit.
